# Insights

## Executive Summary

The fictional coffee shop chain shows its strongest revenue performance from coffee and breakfast-style pairings. Downtown leads store revenue, while loyalty customers show better purchasing behavior than standard customers.

## Key Findings

1. Downtown is the strongest store by total revenue.
2. Coffee products drive the largest share of sales.
3. Bakery add-ons are important because they frequently appear with coffee orders.
4. Loyalty members have higher repeat activity and stronger total spend.
5. Morning orders are especially important for revenue.

## Recommendations

1. Promote coffee and bakery bundles during morning hours.
2. Use loyalty offers to increase repeat visits from standard customers.
3. Keep best-selling coffee products highly visible on menus and ordering screens.
4. Compare lower-performing stores against Downtown to identify staffing, pricing, or product mix differences.
5. Track product pairings over time to improve cross-sell campaigns.

## Next Improvements

- Add a dashboard in Tableau, Power BI, or Looker Studio.
- Expand the dataset to include more dates and seasonal trends.
- Add inventory data to analyze product waste and stock planning.
- Add customer demographics for deeper segmentation.
